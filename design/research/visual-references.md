## What the references collectively say

- **Persistent object, many readings.** The strongest precedents (1, 2) keep one mark on screen and re-read it, instead of cutting between charts. Of our three concepts, COIL takes this furthest.
- **Retail precedent is merchant-facing and event-driven (3, 6).** That points at the calendar: BFCM, holiday and promo windows, which are exactly the variation our data really has.
- **Honesty conventions (4, 8)** shape the comparison rules in `approved/`.
