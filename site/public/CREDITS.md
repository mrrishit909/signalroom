URL). Versions below were checked against `site/package-lock.json` and

**Deliberately not used.**
- **GSAP:** its "Standard no-charge" license is free but not OSI-approved, and Motion covers the needs.
- **Three.js:** no 3D is needed.
- **No images, icons or sound assets.** All marks are drawn from data. The
  Vite scaffold's `public/icons.svg` was removed as dead weight (unused once
  the scaffold `App.tsx` was replaced); `public/favicon.svg` was replaced
  with a small drawn coil mark in the site's own palette, so no Vite credit
  is owed there.

**Data.** Synthetic, generated by this repo (`data/generate_seed_data.py`, seed 20260101). No third-party data.

**Design references** (inspiration only, nothing copied) are listed in `design/research/visual-references.md`.

**License texts.** React, React DOM, D3, and Motion are MIT/ISC, permissive
and requiring only this attribution notice; full license texts ship with
each package under `site/node_modules/<package>/LICENSE` and are not
duplicated here. The three OFL-1.1 fonts likewise ship their full license
text alongside the font files in `site/node_modules/@fontsource*/<package>/LICENSE`
and are bundled into `site/dist` by the @fontsource packages themselves.
